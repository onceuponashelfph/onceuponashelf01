About Once Upon A Shelf

Every Great Story Begins with a Single Page...

ONCE UPON A SHELF started from a shared love for reading—a simple space made for people who enjoy getting lost in good books, finding new favorites, and taking time to slow down with a story.

Our Story & Meaning

Why We Built This Space

What began as a small bookstore idea grew into ONCE UPON A SHELF because we wanted a place where readers can easily browse, discover, and appreciate different kinds of books. Whether someone is looking for a quiet escape, a fresh perspective, or a book to finish over the weekend, we wanted every visit to feel welcoming and unhurried.

Behind Our Name

Our name reflects the quiet magic of books waiting to be discovered. Every shelf holds adventures, lessons, and memorable stories ready for the right reader to open.
